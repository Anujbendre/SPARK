# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making SPARK a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive open-source project. We want everyone, regardless of their experience level or background, to feel comfortable participating, learning, sharing ideas, and contributing.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, kind, and constructive.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Giving helpful feedback on issues and pull requests.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences, and approaches.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, threatening, or humiliating others.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take appropriate corrective action, including warnings or temporary or permanent restrictions on participation, depending on the severity of the behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all SPARK project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication channels. It also applies when someone represents SPARK or its community in public spaces.
